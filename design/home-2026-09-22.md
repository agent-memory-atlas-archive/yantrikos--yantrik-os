## Living here

It feels like a desk where every drawer is labelled and the labels are mostly true.
`describe` is the best thing on the machine. When an app refuses, it says exactly why
and what would work: the shelved spreadsheet, yDoc's "original file is gone", the
mode-chip refusal, the Dentist denial ("this is an answer, not an error"). I never had
to guess what a verb did. Notes and Files feel finished: every write came back with
`matches_disk: true` or a true notice, in ~100 ms.

Where it got in the way:

- **I couldn't see my own desktop.** Once four or five app windows are open, the shell's
  screens (About, System, Settings, Notifications, Bond, launcher) render *underneath*
  them. `show_screen` reports success, and neither I nor a mind can close, minimise or
  raise anything to get there. A person would click the ×; the published surface has no ×.
- **Approval cards are thin on meaning.** The terminal card is excellent (caller, pid,
  kernel-verified program, the command, 310 chars in full). The delete card names a UUID.
  What makes a delete dangerous is exactly the part it leaves out.
- **Files and documents don't talk.** Moving a file under an open document strands
  the document, and the escape hatch (`open`) silently drops work.
- **Notifications are a one-way street for minds.** A mind can post but can't dismiss or
  mark-read by id, because the CLI turns "67" into 67.
- **The companion talks to itself.** Its proactive notifications are clipped to one line,
  one of them was an error message, and the memory it reflects on is 49 % one repeated
  network event. It told me so itself yesterday (id 60: "~98% of it is me talking to
  myself").

What I reached for that wasn't there: a reminder field on events; a way to close a
window; a spreadsheet (shelved, and it says so); any Northwind mail, which the Overnight
note implies should exist; a visible "Hermes is working…" anywhere but a small teal dot
beside "Yantrik" in the top bar.

What surprised me: how far this has come since OS-AUDIT (17–18 Sep). Uptime,
WiFi, the empty System values, the dead notification daemon, DND persistence and the
52.7 % idle burn are all fixed, and I verified each against `/proc`, `busctl` or
`settings.yaml`.

What I would keep: `describe`/`act` with grades, and the refusals' wording. The
kernel-verified line on the approval card ("the kernel said so, not the caller"). Notes'
`matches_disk`. The reminder that arrived on time.

## Findings worth an issue

1. **The card asking me to delete an event didn't say which event.** *Observed:*
   `delete_event {id}` card shows only `id: 01a0c718-3931-…` (`05-delete-card.png`).
   *Why:* the card renders the call's arguments, and for id-addressed actions those mean
   nothing to a person; this is the one card where the person most needs the title and
   time. *Size:* S. Resolve id → "Dentist · Fri 25 Sep 13:00" in the card (calendar can
   describe it).
2. **A mind's approval call hung for five minutes after the card had gone.** *Observed:*
   bridge polled card #68 for 60 s (06:48:18→06:49:17), then silence until Hermes' 300 s
   timeout; the later card #97 was handled correctly at 110 s. *Why:* suspected, in
   yos-mcp's poll loop when a second tool call runs concurrently (Hermes issued
   `os_describe` + `os_act` in parallel on that turn). Not proven. *Size:* M.
3. **Switching to About, System or Settings showed me nothing, because the apps were on
   top.** *Observed:* `show_screen` → `settled: True`, screenshots show the Editor
   (`10-about.png`, `10-system.png`); `focus_window title=Yantrik` → no match; no
   close/minimise action exists. *Why:* the shell is a layer below app windows and the
   surface can't raise it or move windows. *Size:* M.
4. **The launcher never opens.** *Observed:* `open_app name=launchpad` → "launching",
   nothing visible, `failed_launches: []`; `show_screen launchpad` → no such screen.
   *Why:* unknown; listed as a screen but not in `show_screen`'s list. *Size:* S.
